# Faction notes

## Necropolis

- **Core army: Vampire Lords and Dread Knights.** Ghost Dragons are optional. Dismiss Walking Dead and Zombies; they slow the army and add nothing.
- **Vampire Lords** take no retaliation and drain life: the damage they deal revives and heals them, capped by the target's HP. They cannot drain undead, elementals, golems, gargoyles or mechanical units; feed them on weak living stacks.
  - Week 1 (about four of them): too few to fight alone. Keep them with the army and do not let them take permanent losses.
  - From week 2: a stack large enough to absorb a hit can fight several enemy stacks at once, as long as drain revives faster than they die. Use the map edge and obstacles so only one or two enemy stacks can reach it. A defense buff helps.
  - Use hit-and-run when the enemy is dangerous: wait, strike last, move first next round.
  - They are only speed 9; pick up speed artifacts.
  - Beware enemy heroes with high Spell Power and Lightning or Implosion: they can kill Vampire Lords faster than drain revives them.
- **Power Liches** shoot a death cloud that does not hurt undead. Shoot enemy shooters first. They are slow (speed 7) and fragile; keep wounded ones out of reach.
- **Animate Dead** returns killed undead permanently; cast it before the last enemy stack dies. Thant specializes in it.
- **Death Ripple** damages every living, non-dragon unit and spares an all-undead army.
- **Necromancy** raises Skeletons from the dead after a battle. Skeletons are slow: hire a second hero to follow the main one and take them.
- Skeleton snowballing needs a large map; on a small one it does not pay.
- The **Cloak of the Undead King** set (cloak, amulet, boots) raises Liches instead of Skeletons; collect it if the map has it.
- Mixing living creatures into an undead army costs the living ones morale; decline them.

## Conflux

- **Carry only Phoenixes.** At speed 21 they give the most movement, they fly over everything, they are immune to fire, and a Phoenix strike plus its retaliation wipes most low-level stacks.
- With a Grail (Aurora Borealis) every spell is in the Mage Guild.
- **Armageddon** is the win button for a Phoenix army: it hits every unit for a lot and Phoenixes take nothing. One cast clears most garrisons; several casts, while the Phoenixes stay out of reach, beat armies many times your size. Do not cast it on fights two normal exchanges win.
- Grow **Spell Power and Knowledge** (Garden of Revelation, artifacts, Sorcery, Intelligence); refill at a Magic Spring each week.
- Leave Magic, Energy and Magma Elementals in the garrisons for defense.
- The University teaches magic schools: Earth (Town Portal, Resurrection), Air (Fly, Dimension Door).
- Firebirds are fire-immune too: Armageddon does nothing to them; use Implosion or plain attacks.
